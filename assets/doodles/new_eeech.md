# The new EEEch plan

- Control characters 00-1F
  - 00 Unknown
  - 01 Clear screen
  - 02 Clear line
  - 03 Backspace
  - 04 Newline
  - 05 Tab
  - 06 Space
- Puncuation 20-2F
  - 21 Period
  - 22 Comma
  - 23 Colon
  - 24 Semi colon
  - 25 Question mark
  - 26 Exclamation mark
- Alphanum 30-5F
  - 30-39 0-9
  - 3A-43 a-z
  - 44-5E A-Z
- Comparisons 60-6F
  - 60 Less than
  - 61 Equal
  - 62 Greater than
  - 63 Not equal
- Symbols 70-8F
  - 70 Tilde
  - 71 Hashtag
